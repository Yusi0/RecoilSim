1. THREE RECOMMENDATION POLICIES SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Policy A: Balanced Hurdle (Current Benchmark)]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Definition:
   - Evaluates all candidate attachment combinations (Barrel x Underbarrel).
   - Applies 4 absolute Practicality Guard thresholds before role selection:
     * deltaTTK < 20.0 ms       (TTK delay < 1.2 frames @ 60Hz)
     * deltaADS < 35.0 ms       (Aim settle latency delay < 2 frames)
     * deltaSprint < 45.0 ms    (Sprint-to-fire response delay < 2.7 frames)
     * deltaAimWalk < 1.10 s/s  (Aim-walk speed penalty < 1.1 studs/s)
   - Candidates violating ANY hurdle are hard-rejected.
   - Surviving candidates are partitioned into Pareto frontiers per role:
     * Overall:    Minimizes [R95, |meanX|, |meanY|]
     * Vertical:   Minimizes [|meanY|, sigmaY]
     * Horizontal: Minimizes [|meanX|, sigmaX]
   - Anchor selection picks the non-dominated top candidate for each role.

2. Strengths:
   - Guaranteed practical safety: complete elimination of troll builds (Muffler,
     Oil Filter, Romanian Grip on slow weapons).
   - Clean, predictable recommendations.

3. Weaknesses:
   - Does not preserve trade-off awareness: a candidate offering an extreme recoil
     benefit with a borderline penalty is silently discarded.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Policy B: Pareto Only (No Hard Hurdles)]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Definition:
   - Absolutely NO hard penalty thresholds.
   - Evaluates candidates across multi-dimensional objective vectors combining
     recoil metrics and all 4 penalty metrics:
     * 5D Vector: [R95, max(0, deltaTTK), max(0, deltaADS), max(0, deltaSprint), max(0, deltaAimWalk)]
     * 7D Vector: [R95, sigmaX, sigmaY, max(0, deltaTTK), max(0, deltaADS), max(0, deltaSprint), max(0, deltaAimWalk)]
   - Candidate A dominates Candidate B iff A <= B in all dimensions and A < B in at least one.
   - Keeps ALL non-dominated candidates.
   - Runs role selection directly on the non-dominated set.

2. Strengths:
   - Strictly mathematical: zero arbitrary thresholds, zero composite weights.
   - Retains all genuine trade-off possibilities.

3. Fatal Failure Case (Discovered in Audit):
   - In M231, "Muzzle Brake + Romanian Grip" achieves deltaR95 = -1.77 (vs MB solo -1.58).
     Because its R95 is 0.19 studs lower, NO other candidate dominates it in the R95 axis.
   - Consequently, in Policy B, MB + Romanian BECOMES THE #1 OVERALL RECOMMENDATION,
     inflicting a disastrous +60.2 ms (3.6 frame) sprint-to-fire penalty!
   - Furthermore, "Oil Filter + Romanian" (inflicting +122.7 ms / 7.4 frame delay)
     becomes the #1 Horizontal recommendation!
   - 35 out of 36 M231 candidates (97.2%) remain in the 7D frontier.
   - Conclusion: Pareto dominance ALONE fails to compress the candidate pool and
     promotes unplayable extreme builds to top rank.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Policy C: Pareto + Extreme Penalty Guard (Recommended Policy)]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Definition:
   - Step 1 (Pareto Pruning):
     Eliminate all mathematically dominated candidates across the 7D space.
     (Eliminates candidates that have worse recoil AND worse/equal penalties).
   - Step 2 (Extreme Practicality Guard):
     Apply Balanced Hurdle thresholds (20ms TTK, 35ms ADS, 45ms Sprint, 1.1s/s Walk)
     as an extreme penalty guard to determine primary recommendations.
   - Step 3 (Trade-off Preservation & Flagging):
     Candidates that are Pareto Non-Dominated BUT exceed a practicality guard are
     NOT permanently erased; they are tagged with [FLAG_HURDLE].
   - Step 4 (Role Selection):
     Select primary recommendations (Overall, Vertical, Horizontal) exclusively from
     the clean (Guard-passed) Pareto set.
   - Step 5 (UI Exposure):
     Expose [FLAG_HURDLE] candidates in secondary tabs / insight badges as
     "Extreme Recoil Specialization (Caution: Sprint latency +X ms)".

2. Strengths:
   - Combines the mathematical rigor of Pareto optimization with the practical
     safety of Balanced Hurdles.
   - Restores M231 recommendations to healthy combat builds (MB solo, Compensator)
     while transparently documenting genuine trade-off candidates.

3. CONVERSION ATTACHMENT ARCHITECTURE & AUDIT FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. Status in Candidate Pool:
   - In the 69,519 candidate combinations across 292 firearms, conversion attachments
     were 100% EXCLUDED from Layer 2 candidate generation.
   - Reason: candidateSlots is strictly hardcoded to ['Barrel', 'Underbarrel'].
   - Conversions exist in the 'Ammo' slot (or 'Other' slot) and therefore act as
     Layer 1 Context attachments.

2. Physical Impact of Conversions:
   - Conversions mutate the fundamental baseline of the firearm:
     * Caliber & Damage: e.g. Beowulf ECR (49 dmg -> 5.56 conversion)
     * RPM: e.g. M231 Heavy Buffer (1225 RPM -> 250 RPM)
     * Magazine Capacity: e.g. M231 M855 (30 rounds -> 100 round drum)
     * Recoil Tables: e.g. Colt SMG Heavy Buffer modifies base recoil structures.

3. The Baseline Dilemma & The Heavy Buffer Proof:
   - If the Practicality Guard calculates deltaTTK, deltaADS, deltaSprint against
     the Pure Stock firearm (loadCompiledWeaponData(weaponId, {})):
     * M231 Heavy Buffer drops RPM from 1225 to 250 (TTK rises from 146.9ms to 720.0ms).
     * The deltaTTK vs Pure Stock is +573.1 ms!
     * Because +573.1 ms >> 20.0 ms, EVERY SINGLE BARREL AND GRIP (including stock None)
       FAILS THE PRACTICALITY GUARD!
     * The candidate pool becomes 100% EMPTY (0 candidates remaining).
   - If the Practicality Guard calculates deltas against the Context Baseline
     (loadCompiledWeaponData(weaponId, contextAttachments)):
     * The conversion's new stat profile (250 RPM, 720ms TTK) becomes the 0-point baseline.
     * Muzzle Brake RPM is 250 -> deltaTTK = 0.0 ms (PASS).
     * Muzzle Brake + Romanian sprint penalty (+60.2 ms) is correctly detected (FAIL).
     * The candidate pool functions with 100% mathematical integrity.

4. Architectural Decision:
   - Rule: The Baseline for Practicality Guards MUST ALWAYS BE the Context Baseline:
     baseData = loadCompiledWeaponData(weaponId, contextAttachments)
   - Conversions must NEVER be mixed into Layer 2 candidate combinations.
   - Workflow:
     [Step 1] User selects Weapon and Desired Conversion (Ammo/Other slot).
     [Step 2] System locks Conversion into Layer 1 Context.
     [Step 3] System establishes Context Baseline.
     [Step 4] System runs Policy C (Pareto + Practicality Guard) to optimize Barrel & Underbarrel.
